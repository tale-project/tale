pages and their German and French counterparts, 381 pages in total. Each path
has a recorded whole-page review and source basis in the external session
ledger. The 33 video pages were excluded and unchanged. READMEs, their templates,
authoring skills and the screenshot gallery were also reviewed.

untouched-tree catalogue round. The previous PR checks were green. A coverage

### R3-1 · Long page titles

The header now allows long words to wrap within its available width. A closing
visual read also shortened the German title so it reads naturally on a phone.
Shared typography is retained. Three locale-specific phone regressions in
`tests/e2e/specs/page-header.spec.ts` pin the document and heading widths.

### R3-2 · Browser-test URL overrides

`packages/e2e/src/config.ts` now applies the environment target after the
service default. Three regression tests cover environment precedence, explicit
service URL and the port fallback. This made the documented existing-server
workflow behave as described.

### R3-3 · Remaining content and language gaps

Completed the missing families, reread all other pages, and rewrote literal
API prose without removing endpoint detail. Corrected backup/restore boundaries,
provider configuration, role permissions, indexing, upload retry rules and
workflow-dependent task outputs against source and live local probes. Commercial
certifications were retained on the owner's confirmation; unverified commercial
terms are not inferred from the open-source code.

### R3-4 · Links survive the rewrite

Preserved incoming section IDs and normalized German/French page links. New
`anchors.test.ts` uses the actual heading extractor; `links.test.ts` rejects
missing locale prefixes. The authoring contract now names these checks. A rendered-link sweep also found
21 missing targets in the automation catalog and trigger guides: bare headings
inside `Steps` were consumed as separate children. Explicit `Step` wrappers
retain each instruction and its image together. `structure-steps.test.ts`
uses the real renderer to require titled steps and preserve their rich content.

## Latest-main integration

## Not reached

Video content, production infrastructure restores, real external provider quality,
enterprise commercial operations and exhaustive native-device behavior were not
part of this round. The source-based instructions distinguish prerequisites and
provider-dependent results instead of promising unobserved outcomes.
