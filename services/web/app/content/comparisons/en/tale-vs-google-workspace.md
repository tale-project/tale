## Should AI follow your apps or your project board?

For a company already working in Google Workspace, the first AI purchase may be an extension of familiar documents, email, and meetings. A separate project workspace needs a clear purpose: coordinating work that several people and agents contribute to over time. Start with that operating decision, not a list of AI features.

[Google Workspace with Gemini](https://workspace.google.com/solutions/ai/) places AI in applications including Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and Meet. Its current offering also describes Workspace Studio for creating workflows across Workspace apps. The suite is therefore relevant to both everyday assistance and automation, not just document drafting.

## Evaluate where colleagues already work

Consider Google Workspace with Gemini when most of the work should happen inside your existing productivity suite. Test the applications and edition your team actually uses, including access to source files and the way colleagues edit or challenge generated material. Include automation requirements rather than assuming they require another product.

Consider Tale when the missing piece is a shared project structure for planning, assigning tasks to people or configured agents, and reviewing the resulting work. [Projects](https://docs.tale.dev/platform/projects/overview) bring tasks, files, and conversations together around the same outcome. Provider and deployment choices are additional considerations. Introducing a new workspace makes sense only if that coordination improves the team's process enough to justify another working environment.

## Evaluate a meeting-to-delivery cycle

Use fictional workshop notes that call for a customer FAQ, a launch checklist, and one unresolved decision. Ask the team to produce the two deliverables, assign the decision, and review the results after a midweek requirement change.

Check where ownership is visible, whether the reviewer can find the original context, and how follow-up tasks survive beyond the meeting. Include the effort to move or connect documents without duplicating the source of truth. Compare the complete weekly cycle in each setup.
