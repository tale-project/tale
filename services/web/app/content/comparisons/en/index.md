## Choose the layer your team needs

Tale is a shared project workspace for people and configured AI agents. Compare team platforms when task ownership, delegation, and review are your main decision. Look at assistants and specialized tools when your team needs help inside an existing process. Frameworks are a build-versus-adopt decision; compatible runtimes are part of an execution setup.

These options can complement each other. Start with one real project and check where the brief, source files, current owner, and reviewed result will live.

## Test a complete handoff

Give both options the same brief. Ask a teammate to change a requirement, inspect the evidence, request a revision, and pick the work up a week later. Include permissions, setup effort, and the tools needed to produce your actual deliverables.

[Choose an evaluation task](/use-cases), explore [Tale projects](/platform/projects), or [request a demo](/request-demo) with your own brief.

## How to read these guides

The guides compare documented scope and recognize genuine overlaps. Each links to official sources and proposes an evaluation task. Research was reviewed on 3 October 2026; the alternatives were not installed or benchmarked. Check each product's current availability, licensing, and maintenance status before adopting it.

The [Flowise guide](/compare/tale-vs-flowise) covers its announced end of life, and the [Vibe Kanban guide](/compare/tale-vs-vibe-kanban) covers its maintenance transition.
