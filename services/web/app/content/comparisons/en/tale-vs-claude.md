## Compare the whole work environment

Claude Enterprise is a relevant alternative when a company wants AI to contribute to research, documents, coding, and recurring work. The useful buying question is how your team will coordinate those contributions, including the moment another person needs to challenge or take over a result.

[Claude Enterprise](https://claude.com/solutions/enterprise) includes Chat, Claude Code, and Cowork. Anthropic describes Cowork as handling delegated work and documents connectors to company tools, plus organization controls. Evaluate that current scope rather than reducing the offering to conversational assistance.

## Compare at a glance

| Criterion | Tale | Claude Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Working surface | Shared projects with assigned tasks, files, and review | Claude Enterprise combines Chat, Claude Code, and Cowork |
| Coordination | Execution results and review are tied to assigned project tasks | Cowork handles delegated work with connectors to company tools |
| Setup choices | Self-hosted or managed operation with supported runtime choices | Connected work experiences with organization controls |

## Decide where responsibility lives

Consider Claude Enterprise when the organization wants to use Claude's work experiences with its connected tools and administrative setup. Test the experiences relevant to your team and the access policies you will actually enable. A coding workflow and a document workflow may need different evaluation cases.

Consider Tale when you want projects and assigned tasks to be the shared structure for teammates and configured agents. [Project agents](https://docs.tale.dev/platform/projects/project-agents) connect agent configuration, execution results, and review to that structure. Provider choice and self-hosted or managed operation are additional criteria, after you confirm that the working process fits.

Tale can also use compatible Anthropic subscriptions through Claude Code for task and automation execution. This does not make every Claude plan or model automatically compatible. Chat requires API credentials; subscription calls bypass Tale gateway spending caps. Verify the supported setup in the [provider guide](https://docs.tale.dev/platform/admin/providers).

## Evaluate an internal campaign handoff

Use a fictional launch brief with source notes, a draft announcement, and a review checklist. Ask for a revised announcement and a separate task to resolve an unsupported claim. Have a different teammate take over that task after the first review.

Assess how each setup preserves the brief, exposes the current responsibility, and keeps review feedback usable for the next contributor. Include the administrator's work to grant the necessary access. [Request a Tale demo](https://tale.dev/request-demo) with this campaign handoff.
