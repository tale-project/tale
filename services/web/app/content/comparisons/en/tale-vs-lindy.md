Where should a request become shared work: in the channels your team already uses, or on a project board that tracks the assignment through delivery? Tale and Lindy both address collaboration, so the answer depends on your team's working habits.

## Compare the collaboration setting

Lindy Enterprise describes an AI teammate working in Slack and across company tools. It distinguishes personal assistance from shared channel work and documents recurring tasks, reusable skills, sources, and approval controls for external actions. Collaboration and governance are already part of its proposition. [Explore Lindy Enterprise](https://www.lindy.ai/enterprise) and its [product guide](https://docs.lindy.ai/).

Tale puts the brief, assigned agent, task discussion, and resulting files inside a shared project. Teammates can organize related work on a board, delegate to configured agents, and inspect progress and outputs. For example, a customer-success team can separate research, a renewal brief, and proposed documentation changes into owned tasks. See [Tale project tasks](https://docs.tale.dev/platform/projects/tasks).

## Test a handoff from a customer conversation

Use an approved meeting summary and a fictional account record. Request a renewal briefing, a list of unanswered questions, and a draft response. Then involve someone from product who needs to verify one claim before the work is finished.

Compare how the new reviewer finds the relevant discussion and sources, changes the brief, and signals that a revision is needed. Include a proposed external update in the test, but examine its approval path before enabling it. In Tale, connector writes use the automation path and its configured rules; ordinary chat is not the execution surface.

## Choose where ownership should live

Evaluate Lindy if bringing an AI teammate into existing Slack conversations and connected tools matches your adoption plan. Evaluate Tale if you want project tasks to be the team's shared record of agent assignments and deliverables. Neither collaboration style is universally better: test whether the people responsible for the outcome can comfortably follow and steer the work.
