## Is the unit of work a person or a shared project?

A capable personal assistant can help one person keep commitments and complete recurring work. A shared project also needs responsibility to remain understandable when someone else takes over. Start by deciding whether you are buying assistance for an individual or a working structure for a team.

On vellum.ai, Vellum presents a [personal AI assistant](https://www.vellum.ai/docs/getting-started/what-is-vellum). Its documentation describes [persistent memory](https://www.vellum.ai/docs/key-concepts/memory-and-context), [recurring scheduled tasks](https://www.vellum.ai/docs/key-concepts/scheduling), [connected services](https://www.vellum.ai/docs/key-concepts/oauth-integrations), and [hosting in Vellum Cloud or locally](https://www.vellum.ai/docs/hosting-options). Vellum also documents a separate [AI development platform](https://docs.vellum.ai/home/getting-started/overview) for building workflows; this page compares the personal assistant. Personal assistance can include business work; the distinction is the organizing model.

## Compare at a glance

| Criterion | Tale | Vellum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting point | A shared project workspace with tasks, owners and review. | A personal AI assistant with persistent memory and recurring tasks. |
| Working context | Project files and conversations support work that teammates can hand over. | Connected tools and personal assistant memory; not the same concept as project context. |
| Pilot focus | Ask a colleague to take over the recurring update and review the next result. | Test the local or cloud assistant workflow and the context it retains. |

## Evaluate continuity and ownership

Consider Vellum when the main goal is an assistant that follows your preferences and handles tasks across your tools. Test its actual [permissions](https://www.vellum.ai/docs/trust-security/the-permissions-model) and the surfaces you will use, including how you inspect a recurring task before relying on it. Local or cloud operation also deserves evaluation on its own terms.

Consider Tale when your team needs one project where people and configured agents receive assignments, track progress, and review results together. Its [project workspace](https://docs.tale.dev/platform/projects/overview) organizes shared tasks, files, and conversations. A recurring job can contribute to that project, but the decision to adopt Tale should come from the team workflow you want to run. Do not assume that personal memory and shared project context have identical behavior.

## Evaluate a handover before a holiday

Create a fictional weekly supplier update with source notes, three open questions, and one recurring reminder. After the first update, have a teammate take responsibility while the original owner is away.

Check what the new owner can discover, which access they need, and how they distinguish completed work from unresolved decisions. Then ask for a correction to the previous update. Compare both the personal assistance experience and the clarity of the handover. [Request a Tale demo](https://tale.dev/request-demo) using this shared operations case.

This comparison describes public documentation reviewed on 5 October 2026; it is not a hands-on benchmark.
