A useful research result connects a question to evidence and a decision. Tale helps your team organize that process: agree on the brief, assign investigations to agents, and review the findings in a shared project.

## Give every investigation a clear question

Create a project for a market assessment, vendor evaluation, customer discovery synthesis, or internal investigation. Add reference files and define the questions, permitted sources, time period, and expected output. Turn independent questions into separate tasks so people can see what is being investigated and what is ready to review.

Project agents work with the tools and sources configured for them. Multiple agents can investigate independent questions within available sandbox capacity. For a synthesis task, explicitly provide the relevant findings and references; separate agent workspaces do not imply shared memory.

## Example: evaluate a new market

| Investigation | Deliverable | Acceptance criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer problems | A summary linked to interviews or supplied research | Each major conclusion names its evidence |
| Existing alternatives | A dated comparison with primary sources | Product facts are separated from interpretation |
| Decision brief | Options, uncertainties, and proposed next steps | The team can trace the recommendation to the findings |

Ask agents to identify missing evidence and conflicting sources as well as supporting material. A fluent report is not proof that a source is reliable or a claim is current.

## Review the reasoning together

Keep questions and revision requests on the task. Teammates can challenge a conclusion, supply another reference, or ask for a narrower analysis without losing the original brief and returned files. A reviewer can check both the sources and the relevance of the recommendation to the team's decision.

Tale fits research that involves several contributors and a result that must be handed over. For a quick isolated question, a search or chat tool may be enough. The project becomes useful when the investigation needs ownership, parallel work, and a visible review process.

Start with one decision your team needs to make. [Organize project knowledge](https://docs.tale.dev/platform/projects/manage-files), explore [the shared task board](/platform/projects), or [request a demo](/request-demo) with your research brief.
