# Blog reading experience

> **Prefix** `BLOG-` · **Reset** none · **Cost** 7 boxes

This suite covers the visual and editorial judgement of the blog index,
articles, diagrams, and downloads. Automated boundaries are recorded in
[automation.md](../reference/automation.md).

## Preconditions

Follow [setup.md](../setup.md). Use the current built marketing site with the complete EN/DE/FR
article set. For an unpublished revision, run the documented marketing-content preview mode.

> **Agent note**: wait for the article heading and images to load before judging; inspect the
> article body and full-size diagram, not only the introductory cover.

## Reading and visual judgement

- [ ] `BLOG-F1` · **Open `/blog`, `/de/blog`, and `/fr/blog` and choose a guide from its cover/title** → the next decision is clear, card copy reads naturally, and each route opens the intended language.
- [ ] `BLOG-F2` · **Read the workflow-choice guide and complete part of its downloaded worksheet** → the filled example explains the difficult fields, assumptions remain visible, and the blank section is usable without reconstructing the article.
- [ ] `BLOG-F3` · **Inspect the deployment and evaluation guides with their tables** → data destinations, time units, totals, column relationships, and illustrative assumptions remain understandable.
- [ ] `BLOG-A1` · **Inspect the index, one article, and its diagram in light/dark at 320, 390, 768, 1024, and 1440px in all three languages** → copy is readable, illustrations are not clipped, table scroll hints are clear, and the full-size diagram makes small labels inspectable.
- [ ] `BLOG-A2` · **Navigate the article outline, source links, diagram link, worksheet link, and related reading using only the keyboard** → focus is visible, the reading order makes sense, and no essential explanation depends on an image.
- [ ] `BLOG-B1` · **Compare the displayed byline, source-review date, illustration caption, and worked scenario with the content** → none implies a named human review, publication date, captured interface, customer result, or measured Tale performance that did not occur.
- [ ] `BLOG-F4` · **Read an article from its title through its final section and apply the questions in `app/content/blog/QUALITY.md`** → the opening answers a concrete question, the example makes the advice usable, and each section helps with the same task.
