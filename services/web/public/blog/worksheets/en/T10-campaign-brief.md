# Campaign research and review pack

Copy this pack for a real campaign. Blank fields are intentional. Label synthetic material as synthetic, and keep customer information within the access approved for the project. A generated persona is a hypothesis aid, not an interviewed customer.

## Filled result-reading exercise

**Separate synthetic exercise, not a completed test.** Assume random assignment to two groups of 1,000 eligible recipients, the same observation window, one click and one qualified-enquiry indicator per recipient, and consistent qualification. For this exercise, assume every qualified enquirer also clicked their assigned message. In real data, the per-clicker numerator must count recipients who both clicked and made a qualified enquiry, rather than all enquirers in the group. Actual campaigns need an appropriate allocation and uncertainty plan.

| Measure | Message A | Message B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assigned eligible recipients | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| Recipients who click | 60 | 40 |
| Recipients with a qualified enquiry | 3 | 4 |
| Click rate | 60/1,000 = 6% | 40/1,000 = 4% |
| Primary outcome rate | 3/1,000 = 0.3% | 4/1,000 = 0.4% |
| Diagnostic enquiries per clicker | 3/60 = 5% | 4/40 = 10% |

**Interpretation:** A gets more clicks; B gets one more qualified enquiry. Seven total enquiries do not justify a confident stable-winner claim without a suitable uncertainty analysis. The per-clicker ratio conditions on behavior affected by the message, so it is not a clean causal comparison. Keep assigned recipients in the primary denominator and log non-delivery separately. No revenue, incremental lift versus no campaign, or AI-versus-human advantage can be inferred from this exercise.

A campaign comparison does not automatically isolate AI's contribution. If creative, audience, medium, and message all change, name the full treatment being compared. Equal spending does not establish random assignment. A message-versus-message test does not by itself establish incremental impact against no advertising.

For a defined outcome, rate = eligible units with that outcome / eligible units in the declared comparison denominator. If the denominator is zero, the rate is undefined, not zero. Unknown outcome counts remain unknown. Do not exclude nonclickers from an assigned-audience outcome rate or stop repeatedly at the first favorable fluctuation without a valid adaptive design.

## Pilot measurement

Record accepted deliverables, active preparation/review/repair time, elapsed delivery time, and unresolved material claims. Keep measured spend and missing cost categories separate. Use [the evaluation scorecard](T08-evaluation-scorecard.md) when comparing configurations. Publish a performance claim only when the actual experiment, comparison, and evidence support it.
