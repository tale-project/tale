Episode 1 toured the workspace; this episode moves into the room your team will actually live in — and runs a full working session. Its core beat is a controlled experiment: the same onboarding question asked twice, once with no context and once with the Q2 support review attached, so you watch a fluent answer and a grounded answer stop being the same thing. Then the grounded thread gets questioned ("which document says that?"), an Arena verdict gets decided with reasons, and a leadership brief lands on the canvas — and gets cut to three bullets with one sentence.
