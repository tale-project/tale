By the end of this episode you'll have used an automation for real: you read the installed triage workflow before trusting it, create a task and watch it get scored and assigned on camera, follow that run through its journal, open the one that failed and trace it to its step — and approve an outbound customer email with your own click, then find that decision in the audit log seconds later.
